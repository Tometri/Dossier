Juice Wrld: Into the Abyss - 2021

Rating: 🍿🍿🍿

To the uninformed, the movie is a disappointing attempt at capturing the essence of Juice Wrld's music and persona. It follows his DRFL tour and his untimely death. Though it sheds some light on his lifestyle, it fails to do justice to his legacy. As others have said, it's a missed opportunity to truly honor his memory. It feels more like taking the lowest moments of his life after success more than a dive into the artistry. Other documentaries have done a better job, such as "Look at Me: XXXTentacion" or "Lil Peep: Everybody's Everything".
