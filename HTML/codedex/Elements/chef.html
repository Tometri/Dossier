Thai Curry

Welcome to the Curry Corner! Not to be confused with the Curry Coroner!

We have Chef Zetrix Von Austin in the studio
First, our ingredients:

  • Onions
  • Peppers
  • Carrots
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Tofu
  • Curry paste
  • Coconut milk

Now, let's get cooking!

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions, peppers, carrots, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
  2. Add curry paste and cook for a minute to release the flavors.
  3. Stir in tofu and cook for a few minutes until it absorbs the flavors.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and bring to a simmer. Let it cook for 10-15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  5. Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy your delicious Thai curry!
